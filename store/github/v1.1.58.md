## v1.1.58 · 本次更新

### Android
- 修复选片时第一张照片点选无效的问题。
- 修复播放设置切换横竖屏时可能崩溃的问题。
- 优化短横屏和大字号下的相框编辑：设置区域可滚动，照片网格保持可见，保存与返回操作更容易触达。
- 优化开源许可页长名称的排版。
- 已在 HarmonyOS 4.3 真机回归公网 SMB / HTTPS WebDAV 的导入、六张选片、保存和播放流程。

### iPhone / iPad
- 优化设置页在窄屏和无障碍大字号下的布局。
- 修复 iPad 横屏大字号时设置列过窄、导航入口与页脚重叠的问题。
- 补测设置保存后的冷启动恢复、有限照片授权，以及天气失败隐藏和联网恢复。

### 安装说明
Android 仍为 **1.1.58（58）**，沿用原签名，可覆盖升级并保留现有数据。下载下方 `PhotoPilot-v1.1.58-58.apk`；`SHA256SUMS.txt` 用于校验文件。已安装 58 的用户无需重复安装。本次仅补充发布说明，安装包未替换。

## English

### Android
- Fixed the first photo sometimes failing to select.
- Fixed a possible crash when rotating playback settings.
- Improved the frame editor in short landscape windows and at larger text sizes: settings scroll independently while the photo grid remains visible.
- Improved wrapping of long open-source licence names.
- Verified public SMB and HTTPS WebDAV import, six-photo selection, saving and playback on a HarmonyOS 4.3 device.

### iPhone / iPad
- Improved Settings layouts on narrow screens and at accessibility text sizes.
- Fixed a collapsed Settings column and overlapping navigation on iPad in landscape at large text sizes.
- Added physical-device checks for settings persistence after relaunch, limited photo access, weather failure hiding and recovery.

The Android version remains **1.1.58 (58)** with the existing signing certificate. Existing installations can be upgraded in place. This release-note update does not replace the APK; users already on 58 do not need to reinstall.

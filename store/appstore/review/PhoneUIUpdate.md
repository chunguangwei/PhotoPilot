## App Review walkthrough

PhotoPilot is a personal photo-frame app. This update improves source selection and phone navigation. Photos and AI prompts are processed on the device. Device photos use system references. NAS photos support on-demand playback and optional local synchronization.

1. Open Photo Library from Sources or from frame creation. Continue through the system photo permission flow, choose accessible photos or an album, and use the visible Back and Continue controls.
2. Create a manual frame, choose photos and confirm playback. In an existing frame, select photos and use Save frame at the bottom.
3. Open AI creation from Frames. Choose Add photos from a source to connect Photo Library, files, folders or NAS. The existing category text and selected folders remain available when returning.
4. Download an AI model when needed, enter categories in English or Chinese, scan and review the proposed photos. Edit the proposed frame names and confirm the selected photos.
5. For NAS testing, use a read-only SMB or WebDAV photo source. Review connection details can be supplied privately in App Store Connect. Open the frame’s storage options to enable optional local synchronization.
6. In Library, use More for search, filter and sort on compact or large-text layouts. The selected-photo action stays available at the bottom.

The iPhone management interface uses the existing portrait policy. Playback has its own orientation behavior. iPad layouts support their existing orientation policy.

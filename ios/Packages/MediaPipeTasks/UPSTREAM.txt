Google MediaPipe Tasks 1.2.3 binary Swift package wrapper.
Source https://github.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe
Revision 02ca022d573c004c463d8c61eb3f8ce17e95a7fc
Sources/MediaPipeTasksCommon are copied unchanged from that revision.

Package.swift retains the official binary URLs and SHA256 checksums and is
adapted for PhotoPilot's UniversalEmbedder direct LiteRT-LM API. Unused products
and dependencies (Audio, Decision, Text, Vision) and blanket -all_load/-ObjC flags
are omitted. The official SDK archives remain unchanged.

rebuild_categories.py verifies both pinned Common archive hashes, extracts the
three required original conversion-category objects, combines them with the
no-op CategoryAnchor.c, and creates MediaPipeRequiredCategories.xcframework.
Sources/MediaPipeCategoryLinkage exposes a standard C reference to the anchor,
which retains these categories without pulling unused recording classes.
Exact input, original member, and output hashes are in categories-provenance.json.
Both architecture outputs have been rebuilt and compared byte-for-byte.
